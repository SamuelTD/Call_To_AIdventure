A chill wind whistles through the shattered archway of the Emerald Maw, carrying with it the faint echo of dripping water and something—or someone—shuffling in the darkness beyond. Moss-clad stones glimmer with phosphorescent lichen, painting the crude carvings of horned guardians in an eerie green glow. The air is thick with the scent of damp earth and old magic, a reminder that this place was once a sanctum of the Verdant Order—long since fallen to ruin and shadow.

Your torchlight dances across rotting tapestries that still bear the sigil of a coiled dragon clutching a gleaming sword. The hushed murmurs of ancient spells seem to linger at the edge of hearing, as if the stones themselves whisper warnings to intruders. Somewhere deeper, the legend says, lies the Emerald Sword—a blade of such power that its emerald core pulses like a heartbeat, capable of turning the tide of any battle.

With each step, your boots clank against cracked flagstones and loose rubble, and the torch’s flame quivers as though afraid. The path forks ahead: one tunnel slopes downward into blackness, the other rises toward a faint, greenish light. The fate of kingdoms may rest on your choice.

Now what do you do?
