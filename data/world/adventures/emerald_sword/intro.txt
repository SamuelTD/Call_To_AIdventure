The heavy stone door groans open behind you, sealing the light of day with a final, echoing thud. No other way than forward now.

A breath of cold, ancient air greets you—stale with the dust of centuries, yet faintly tinged with the scent of sulfur and scorched metal. Before you lies a descending corridor, the stone underfoot smooth from centuries of passage but dusty and littered with small rocks and pebbles.

Your torch flickers nervously in the gloom, shadows dancing along the narrow walls. Every instinct urges caution, but the king’s seal weighs heavy in your satchel, and the Emerald Sword waits for no coward.

The Tomb of Dragonkin has swallowed braver souls than yours.
Now, it breathes you in.
And you take your first step down.

You arrive in a room where on a wall is a big painted portrait of a King.
