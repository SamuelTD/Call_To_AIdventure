The heavy stone door groans closed behind you, sealing the light of day with a final, echoing thud. No other way than forward now.

A breath of cold, ancient air greets you—stale with the dust of centuries, yet faintly tinged with the scent of sulfur and scorched metal. 

Your torch flickers nervously in the gloom, shadows dancing along the narrow walls. Every instinct urges caution, but the king’s seal weighs heavy in your satchel, and the Emerald Sword waits for no coward.

The Tomb of Dragonkin has swallowed braver souls than yours.
Now, it breathes you in.

You stand before a gate.
